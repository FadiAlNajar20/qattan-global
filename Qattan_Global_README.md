MMM COMPANY HISTORY

Founded in 1951 in Amman, Jordan, Qattan started as a family-owned
business specializing in perfumes and cosmetics, becoming one of the
pioneers in the field. Gaining the trust of Jordanian consumers through
its commitment to authenticity, the company initially focused on retail
operations, offering high-quality, original products from leading global
brands. Company Evolution:

1983: Leadership transitioned to the second generation of the family,
maintaining the company's strong reputation and expanding its presence
in the Jordanian market. This era saw the opening of new branches in
Amman and Aqabo's Special Economic Zone. The business model expanded to
include both retail and wholesale operations.

2000: The company further expanded into the home appliance sector. Its
growth in this industry was significant, Qattan became a key supplier to
major home appliance retailers in Jordan, such as Carrefour, SmartBuy,
and Leaders, and established itself as an official distributor for
numerous international brands.

with the introduction of duty-free operations. Qattan opened spacious
branches in Aqaba's premier shopping centers, offering a superior
consumer experience. The company's

product range grew to include a variety of exclusive electrical
appliances, luxury perfumes, cosmetics, and men's fashion from
world-renowned brands such as Pierre Cardin, Louis Féraud, and Balmain.
Current Position: Today, Qattan has evolved into a diversified group,
venturing into new business sectors while enhancing its existing
operations. The company has launched its own brands, including Jefan,
Farcome, and Clivin, in both the Jordanian and regional markets.
Moreover, Qattan Group has secured exclusive distribution rights for
several prestigious international brands, such as Brooks Brothers,
English Home, and Kite Kate. These brands will be available in premium
shopping malls across Jordan, ensuring their accessibility to a wide
customer base. Infrastructure and Workforce:

Storage Facilities: The company boasts expansive storage spaces---2,500
square meters in Amman and 4,000 square meters in Aqaba's Special
Economic Zone. These facilities ensure the efficient handling and
distribution of products.

Distribution Network: Qattan Group has established a robust distribution
network, catering to a diverse clientele across local markets. This
network plays a crucial role in meeting the needs of customers, from

Recognizing the importance of human capital in achieving success, Qattan
Group places significant emphasis on employee development. With a
workforce of 1,000 employees, the company invests in training programs
to equip its staff with the skills required to deliver exceptional
customer service and maintain operational excellence.
